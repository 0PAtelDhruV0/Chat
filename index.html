THE DETAILS MATTER

What protects your privacy?

Encrypted setup: Browser-encrypted WebRTC connection details are temporarily relayed through Firebase. The secret stays in your invitation URL fragment, not server URLs.

Messages and media: WebRTC uses DTLS for messages and DTLS-SRTP for live audio/video. Content is not stored on the server.

Verification: Both people should compare the safety number through another trusted channel. A shared invitation alone cannot prove someone's identity.

Limits: No software is impossible to hack. A compromised device, malicious browser extension, stolen invite, or security bug can expose communications. WebRTC can reveal IP addresses to peers and network infrastructure.
